# Radio Hacking

{{#include ../../banners/hacktricks-training.md}}

Upimaji wa usalama wa redio huchunguza jinsi kifaa kinavyotuma, kupokea na kutafsiri mawimbi yasiyotumia waya. Software-defined radio (SDR) inaweza kusaidia kupata mawimbi, kurekodi sampuli za in-phase/quadrature (I/Q), na kujaribu demodulation na decoding bila kujifunga kwenye hardware maalum ya protocol.<sup>[[1]](#references)</sup>

Mtiririko wa vitendo ni kutambua band ya frequency na upana wa channel, kunasa vitendo kadhaa vinavyojulikana vya kifaa, kulinganisha mawimbi yanayotokana, kisha kubaini modulation na muundo wa packet. Fanya majaribio ya replay au transmission katika mazingira yaliyotengwa pekee, na kwenye frequencies pamoja na vifaa ambavyo una authorization navyo. Kurasa katika sehemu hii zinahusu RFID, NFC, sub-GHz radio, infrared, BLE na tools zinazohusiana.<sup>[[1]](#references)</sup>
