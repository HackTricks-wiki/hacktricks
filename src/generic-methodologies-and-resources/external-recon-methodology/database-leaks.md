## Injini za Utafutaji za Data Breach

Kwa ufikiaji wa API na tahadhari mahususi za huduma unapotafuta data za breach au reputation, angalia [katalogi ya API za Reconnaissance na Threat-Intelligence](reconnaissance-and-threat-intelligence-apis.md).

- [GreyNoise Visualizer](https://viz.greynoise.io/) - Tafuta IPs na CIDRs, na chunguza shughuli za scanner kwa kutumia tags, CVEs na metadata.<sup>[[1]](#references)</sup>
- [DeHashed](https://www.dehashed.com/) - Tafuta data zilizo wazi kwa kutumia usernames, anwani za email, anwani za IP na vigezo vingine; huduma za monitoring na API pia zinapatikana.<sup>[[2]](#references)</sup>
- [Have I Been Pwned?](https://haveibeenpwned.com/) - Angalia kama anwani ya email inaonekana katika data breaches zinazojulikana au rekodi za paste; notifications na API pia zinapatikana.<sup>[[3]](#references)</sup>
- [ScamSearch](https://scamsearch.io/) - Tafuta rekodi za walaghai zilizoripotiwa kwa kutumia picha ya wasifu, email, username, nambari ya simu, anwani ya crypto au website.<sup>[[4]](#references)</sup>
- [Intelligence X](https://intelx.io/) - Tafuta kwa vigezo kama anwani za email, domains, URLs, IPs na CIDRs katika vyanzo vilivyoindexiwa.<sup>[[5]](#references)</sup>
- [SpyCloud](https://spycloud.com/check-your-exposure/) - Angalia email au domain ya biashara ili kubaini credentials zilizo wazi, utambulisho ulioambukizwa na infostealer, na session cookies zilizoibwa.<sup>[[6]](#references)</sup>
- [WeLeakInfo](https://weleakinfo.io/) - Tafuta databases zilizoleak kwa kutumia domains, majina, emails, IDs, simu, IPs, URLs au hashes.<sup>[[7]](#references)</sup>
- [BreachDirectory](https://breachdirectory.org/) - Angalia kama email au username yako imeathiriwa.
- [LeakCheck](https://leakcheck.io/) - Tafuta data zilizo wazi za email, username, simu, hash au domain na ufuatilie entries mpya.<sup>[[8]](#references)</sup>
- [Findemail.io](https://findemail.io/) - Tafuta anwani za email za kampuni fulani.
- [Library of Leaks](https://search.libraryofleaks.org/) - Tafuta nyaraka za umma, kampuni na watu, ikiwemo datasets za leak.<sup>[[9]](#references)</sup>
- [LeakRadar](https://leakradar.io/) - Tafuta credentials zilizoleak kwa kutumia email, domain au string ghafi na ufuatilie exposures mpya.<sup>[[10]](#references)</sup>
- [InfoStealers](https://infostealers.info/en/info) - Tafuta logs za infostealer kutoka kwenye vifaa vilivyoambukizwa na ufuatilie data mpya.<sup>[[11]](#references)</sup>
- [Leak-Lookup](https://leak-lookup.com/) - Tafuta katika data breaches na ufuatilie credentials zilizo wazi.<sup>[[12]](#references)</sup>
- [Scylla.so](https://scylla.so/) - Injini ya utafutaji ya database breaches inayoendeshwa na jumuiya.
- [Leaked.domains](https://leaked.domains/) - Tafuta credentials zilizoleak na rekodi zinazohusiana kwa kutumia domain, email, username, password, IP na vigezo vingine.<sup>[[13]](#references)</sup>
- [WhiteIntel](https://whiteintel.io/) - Fuatilia shughuli za dark web, credential leaks, data za infostealer na kutajwa kwa brand.<sup>[[14]](#references)</sup>
- [PSBDMP](https://psbdmp.ws/) - Jukwaa la kutafuta na kufuatilia dump za Pastebin.

## Zana za enumerate data leaks

- [Leaker](https://github.com/vflame6/leaker) - CLI ya passive leak discovery inayotafuta katika vyanzo vingi vya mtandaoni kwa kutumia email, username, domain, keyword au simu.<sup>[[15]](#references)</sup>
