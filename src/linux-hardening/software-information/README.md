# Taarifa za Programu

{{#include ../../banners/hacktricks-training.md}}

Programu zilizosakinishwa huongeza hooks za uthibitishaji, violesura vya usimamizi na tabia mahususi za huduma kwenye host ya Linux. Tambua component iliyosakinishwa na privileges zake, kisha kagua ukurasa husika:

- [Huduma za ndani za wavuti na uthibitishaji](local-web-and-auth-services.md) inaeleza nyuso za wavuti, proxy, PAM, LDAP, barua pepe na CI zilizo upande wa host.
- [Hifadhidata na nyenzo za siri](databases-and-secret-material.md) inahusu endpoints za DB za ndani, usanidi wa programu, key stores na tokens.
- [PAM: Moduli za Uthibitishaji Zinazoweza Kuchomekwa](pam-pluggable-authentication-modules.md) inahusu moduli za uthibitishaji za Linux na usanidi wake.
- [FreeIPA pentesting](freeipa-pentesting.md) inahusu deployment za usimamizi wa utambulisho.
- [Logstash](logstash.md) inahusu nyuso za mashambulizi mahususi kwa huduma.
- [LPE na persistence ya Splunk](splunk-lpe-and-persistence.md) inahusu njia za escalation ya ndani na persistence katika deployment za Splunk.
- [Matumizi mabaya ya Node inspector na CEF debugger](electron-cef-chromium-debugger-abuse.md) inahusu violesura vya utatuzi vilivyo wazi.
- [Bypass ya uthibitishaji wa Android rooting-framework manager na hooks za syscall](../../mobile-pentesting/android-app-pentesting/android-rooting-frameworks-manager-auth-bypass-syscall-hook.md) inahusu escalation kutoka app ya ndani hadi root kwenye vifaa vya Android ambavyo tayari vime-rootiwa; mahitaji yake mahususi ya Android yanapatikana kwenye Mobile Pentesting.
