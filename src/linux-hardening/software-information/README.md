Programu zilizosakinishwa huongeza hooks za uthibitishaji, violesura vya usimamizi, na tabia mahususi za huduma kwenye host ya Linux. Tambua component iliyosakinishwa na privileges zake, kisha pitia ukurasa husika:

- [Huduma za wavuti za ndani na uthibitishaji](local-web-and-auth-services.md) huorodhesha maeneo ya host yanayohusu wavuti, proxy, PAM, LDAP, barua pepe, na CI.
- [Databases na nyenzo za siri](databases-and-secret-material.md) huchunguza endpoints za DB za ndani, usanidi wa programu, key stores, na tokens.
- [PAM: Pluggable Authentication Modules](pam-pluggable-authentication-modules.md) huchunguza modules za uthibitishaji na usanidi wa Linux.
- [FreeIPA pentesting](freeipa-pentesting.md) huchunguza deployments za usimamizi wa utambulisho.
- [Logstash](logstash.md) huchunguza maeneo ya mashambulizi mahususi kwa huduma.
- [Splunk LPE and persistence](splunk-lpe-and-persistence.md) huchunguza njia za escalation ya ndani na persistence katika deployments za Splunk.
- [Node inspector and CEF debugger abuse](electron-cef-chromium-debugger-abuse.md) huchunguza violesura vya utatuzi vilivyo wazi.
- [Android rooting framework manager auth bypass and syscall hooks](android-rooting-frameworks-manager-auth-bypass-syscall-hook.md) huchunguza stack hiyo maalumu ya programu inayotegemea Linux.
